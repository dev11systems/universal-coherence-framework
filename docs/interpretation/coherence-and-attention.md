# Coherence and Attention

**(Interpretation Document · Signal Prioritization, Focus, Stability, and the Architecture of Awareness)**

This document explores **attention** as the mechanism by which systems allocate coherence. Attention determines which signals matter, which are ignored, and how internal and external information is organized.

Attention is coherence choosing a target.

---

# 1. Attention as Coherence Allocation

Attention selects:

* which signals are amplified,
* which signals are suppressed,
* which patterns become meaningful,
* how resources are distributed.

Attention = coherence deciding what to stabilize.

---

# 2. The Four States and Attention

### **Chaos — Attentional Collapse**

Markers:

* overwhelm,
* fragmentation,
* hypervigilance,
* inconsistent focus.

Attention cannot stabilize coherence under Chaos.

### **Tension — Conflicted or Unstable Attention**

Markers:

* indecision,
* frequent switching,
* competing priorities,
* cognitive friction.

Tension is where attentional control is negotiated.

### **Flow — Stable and Adaptive Attention**

Markers:

* deep focus,
* sustained clarity,
* flexible shifting,
* integrated awareness.

Flow reflects coherent attention.

### **Unity — Shared or Expanded Attention**

Markers:

* group synchrony,
* mutual presence,
* collective insight,
* altered states of expanded awareness.

Unity aligns attention across agents.
