# Coherence and Perception

**(Interpretation Document · Signals, Filtering, Attention, and the Construction of Experience)**

This document explores **perception** as the process by which systems detect, filter, interpret, and stabilize information. Perception is not raw sensation — it is coherence-making in real time.

What a system perceives is what it can organize.

---

# 1. Perception as Coherence Filtering

Perception requires:

* sensory input,
* predictive models,
* filtering mechanisms,
* attention,
* interpretation.

Perception = coherence applied to the sensory world.

---

# 2. The Four States and Perception

### **Chaos — Perceptual Breakdown**

Markers:

* sensory overwhelm,
* hallucination-like noise,
* disorientation,
* threat-biased perception.

Chaos destabilizes perceptual coherence.

### **Tension — Ambiguous or Conflicted Perception**

Markers:

* optical illusions,
* uncertain interpretation,
* conflicting cues,
* emotional distortion.

Tension reveals the edges of perception.

### **Flow — Accurate and Adaptive Perception**

Markers:

* clarity,
* stable interpretation,
* contextual awareness,
* efficient prediction.

Flow maintains perceptual stability.

### **Unity — Integrated or Expanded Perception**

Markers:

* deep presence,
* synchronized perception with others,
* altered states of consciousness,
* multi-perspective understanding.

Unity expands perceptual coherence.
