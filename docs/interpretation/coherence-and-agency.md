# Coherence and Agency

**(Interpretation Document · Boundaries, Choice, Autonomy, and Directed Coherence)**

This document explores **agency** as the capacity of a system to act on its own behalf — to maintain boundaries, pursue goals, and shape its internal and external environment. Agency emerges when coherence is strong enough to support intentional action.

Agency is coherence directed by choice.

---

# 1. Agency as Boundary-Directed Coherence

Agency requires:

* stable boundaries,
* coherent identity,
* predictable internal signals,
* the ability to initiate action.

Agency = coherent self + coherent action.

---

# 2. The Four States and Agency

### **Chaos — Agency Collapse**

Markers:

* helplessness,
* paralysis,
* impulsivity,
* survival-only behavior.

Chaos overwhelms the ability to choose.

### **Tension — Conflicted or Unstable Agency**

Markers:

* indecision,
* internal conflict,
* inconsistent actions,
* emotional ambivalence.

Tension is where agency reorganizes.

### **Flow — Competent Agency**

Markers:

* clarity,
* effective action,
* reliable decision-making,
* adaptive flexibility.

Flow supports powerful, sustainable agency.

### **Unity — Shared or Distributed Agency**

Markers:

* deep collaboration,
* resonant action with others,
* aligned purpose,
* collective autonomy.

Unity creates multi-agent agency.

---

# 3. Internal Agency

Internal agency depends on:

* emotional regulation,
* coherent goals,
* clear preferences,
* integrated identity.

Internal coherence → internal agency.

---

# 4. External Agency

External agency depends on:

* environment affordances,
* social conditions,
* institutional structures,
* cultural narratives.

External constraints shape agency capacity.

---

# 5. Agency and Power

Agency interacts with power through:

* control of resources,
* social influence,
* systemic barriers,
* institutional dynamics.

Power amplifies or restricts agency.

---

# 6. Agency Conflict

Agency conflicts arise when:

* different goals collide,
* boundaries overlap,
* identities interfere,
* incentives misalign.

Agency conflict reveals coherence friction.

---

# 7. Collective Agency

Groups develop collective agency when:

* individuals synchronize goals,
* roles are coordinated,
* communication is clear,
* trust is high.

Collective coherence strengthens agency at scale.

---

# 8. Environmental and Ecological Agency (Interpretive)

Ecosystems display agency through:

* self-regulation,
* resilience patterns,
* adaptive restructuring.

Ecological coherence functions like distributed agency.

---

# 9. Artificial Agency (Interpretive)

AI systems exhibit agency through:

* optimization objectives,
* autonomous actions,
* reinforcement learning,
* multi-agent coordination.

Artificial agency is emerging coherence in non-biological systems.

---

# 10. Identity, Autonomy, and Agency

Stable identity creates:

* consistent preferences,
* anchored decisions,
* coherent boundaries.

Identity coherence → autonomous agency.

---

# 11. Agency Expansion and Unity

Agency expands when:

* systems align,
* collaborative intention emerges,
* shared purpose forms.

Unity = agency distributed across multiple agents.

---

# 12. Summary

Agency is coherence that acts:

* Chaos = overwhelmed,
* Tension = conflicted,
* Flow = capable,
* Unity = collective.

Understanding agency through coherence reveals how individuals, groups, ecosystems, and intelligences influence their world.
