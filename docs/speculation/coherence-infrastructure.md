# Coherence Infrastructure

**(Speculation Document · Plausible Extensions Built on Foundations)**

This document describes the **structural substrates** that allow coherence to emerge, persist, and propagate across systems. If *coherence protocols* are the patterns of interaction, **coherence infrastructure** is the underlying architecture that makes those patterns possible.

Infrastructure is not an optional feature—it is the *environmental, material, informational, and organizational scaffolding* that determines whether a system can sustain Flow or collapses into Tension and Chaos.

All core mechanisms are grounded in ecology, cybernetics, systems theory, distributed computing, organizational science, and network theory. Speculative sections extend these principles into future cross-intelligence systems.

---

## 1. What Is Coherence Infrastructure?

Coherence infrastructure refers to any structural feature that:

* stabilizes communication,
* distributes memory,
* maintains redundancy,
* reduces drift,
* aligns scales,
* supports synchrony,
* or buffers against perturbation.

It is the **substrate that coherence rests upon**.

Examples:

* ecological scaffolding (keystone species, canopy architecture),
* neural architecture (connectome topology),
* communication networks (bandwidth, routing),
* institutions (shared norms, governance structures),
* distributed AI networks (message-passing substrates).

---

## 2. Why Infrastructure Matters

Without infrastructure:

* protocols fail,
* coherence cannot propagate,
* systems drift toward fragmentation,
* Unity becomes brittle and collapses.

Infrastructure determines:

* how fast coherence can form,
* how robust it is to noise,
* how far it can travel across scales.

It is the **foundation layer** of all adaptive systems.
