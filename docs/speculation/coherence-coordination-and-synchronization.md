## 6. Kuramoto-Type Synchronization Models

Kuramoto models describe oscillators synchronizing under coupling:

* low coupling → incoherence (Chaos),
* medium coupling → partial coherence (Tension),
* tuned coupling → synchrony (Flow),
* high coupling → over-synchrony (Unity → fragility).

This mathematical structure maps cleanly onto UCF.
